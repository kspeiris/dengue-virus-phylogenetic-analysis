# Results

## 1. Dataset Characteristics and Geographic Distribution
A total of 28 complete genomes of human-derived Dengue virus (DENV) isolated from blood samples across Asia were analyzed. The dataset spans 13 countries/territories collected between 1999 and 2023. 

The representative genomes represent all four serotypes:
- **DENV-1**: 7 sequences (Singapore, China, Vietnam, Thailand, Myanmar, India)
- **DENV-2**: 7 sequences (Sri Lanka, Malaysia, India, Pakistan, China, Vietnam, Bangladesh)
- **DENV-3**: 7 sequences (Singapore, Vietnam, Indonesia/Bali, Cambodia, China, India)
- **DENV-4**: 7 sequences (India, Indonesia/Bali, Philippines, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, China)

The average sequence length prior to alignment was approximately **10,650 bp**, and after alignment, the consensus length was **10,825 bp** due to insertions and deletions (indels). An example FASTA entry format from our dataset is shown below:


## 2. Multiple Sequence Alignment Statistics
The MSA generated by MAFFT was evaluated to measure genetic diversity and alignment quality:
- **Total Aligned Positions**: 10,825 bp
- **Constant Sites**: 5,067 sites (46.82% of all sites)
- **Variable (Polymorphic) Sites**: 5,758 sites (53.19% of all sites)
- **Parsimony-Informative Sites**: 5,496 sites (50.77% of all sites)
- **Singleton Sites**: 262 sites (2.42% of all sites)

GC content (excluding gaps) was highly uniform across all 28 genomes, ranging from a minimum of **45.63%** (PQ465587.1, DENV-2 Malaysia) to a maximum of **47.67%** (MG272274.1, DENV-4 India), with an average GC content of **46.60%**. Gaps introduced in the alignment were minimal (average ~1.3% per sequence), indicating high genome homology across serotypes.

## 3. Best-Fit Substitution Model Selection
ModelFinder selected **`TIM2+F+I+R3`** as the best-fit nucleotide substitution model based on all information criteria:
- **AIC Score**: 157,032.409 (TIM2+F+I+R3 ranked #1)
- **BIC Score**: 157,498.944 (TIM2+F+I+R3 ranked #1)

### Substitution Model Properties:
1. **Transitional Rates (Relative to C-G = 1.0000)**:
   - $r_{AC} = r_{AT} = 2.0137$
   - $r_{AG} = 9.3054$ (Transition)
   - $r_{CT} = 29.4081$ (Transition)
   - $r_{CG} = r_{GT} = 1.0000$ (Transversion)
   
   *Note: The C-T transition rate (29.4081) is significantly higher than other rates, indicating a strong transition bias in Dengue genome evolution.*
2. **Empirical Base Frequencies**:
   - $A$: 32.03%, $C$: 20.71%, $G$: 25.87%, $T$: 21.39% (reveals a bias towards adenosine/purines).
3. **Rate Heterogeneity**:
   - **Invariable sites ($I$)**: 30.87% of sites are estimated to be completely static.
   - **FreeRate Model (3 categories)**: 25.76% of sites evolve at a slow rate (0.1474), 32.66% at a moderate rate (1.6740), and 10.71% at a very rapid rate (3.8770).

## 4. Maximum Likelihood Tree Reconstruction
The maximum likelihood tree converged with a log-likelihood ($\ln L$) of **`-78,448.5451`** (standard error: 627.5467). The total tree length was **3.9970** substitutions per site, with internal branches contributing **3.5830** (89.64% of total length), indicating deep phylogenetic signal separating major nodes.

### Tree Topology and Serotype Monophyly:
1. **Four Serotype Clades**: The 28 sequences are partitioned into four monophyletic groups with **100% bootstrap support**, corresponding to DENV-1, DENV-2, DENV-3, and DENV-4.
2. **Sister Groupings**: The DENV-2 and DENV-4 serotype clades share a common ancestral node with **100% support**, indicating they are sister lineages. DENV-3 is sister to the DENV-2/DENV-4 clade, while DENV-1 represents the most basal lineage.
3. **Geographic and Temporal Clustering**:
   - **DENV-1**: Isolate SGEHI(D1)41379Y13 from Singapore (2013) and isolate guangzhou-2014-1 from China (2014) cluster together with **100% support**.
   - **DENV-2**: Shows two major sub-clades: a Sri Lanka (2017)/China (2017) cluster (**100% support**) and an India (2010)/Pakistan (2011) cluster (**100% support**).
   - **DENV-3**: Features a South/East Asia group where isolates from China (GU363549.1) and India (MN253125.1) cluster with **100% support**.
   - **DENV-4**: Displays a cluster of Singapore (2011) and China (2015) isolates (**100% support**) and a separate cluster of Bali (2019) and Thailand isolates (**100% support**).
